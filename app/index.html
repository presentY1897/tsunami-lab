About & how to play

Explore tsunamis on a 3D globe

Tsunami Play is an interactive browser simulation of tsunamis caused by undersea earthquakes and asteroid impacts. Change the source and sea level, watch waves spread across the globe, and explore their effects on coastlines.

Tap the ocean to choose a location. Adjust the earthquake or asteroid settings, optionally select a coast, then choose Create tsunami and press Play. Use the timeline and speed controls to explore the result, or copy a link to share your scenario.

This is an educational simulation using simplified physics and terrain. It is not a tsunami warning system or a tool for real-world flood predictions or evacuation decisions.
